The method

    

      
    
        
  1. Your color: read its hue, saturation and brightness (HSB) as whole numbers.
    2. 
        
  2. Accent: subtract the golden angle, 137.5° (360 − 360 ÷ 1.618), from the hue and take that hue off the color wheel at saturation 100, brightness 95.
    3. 
        
  3. Complementary: keep the hue, bring the saturation down by 61.8% (× 0.382) and raise the brightness 38.2% of the way to 100.
    4. 
        
  4. Contrast: keep the hue and saturation and multiply the brightness by 0.382.
    5. 
        
  5. Proportions: split the design 61.8 / 23.6 / 9.0 / 5.6, each share being the last one divided by φ.
    6. 
      

      
φ = (1 + √5) / 2 ≈ 1.618 · 1/φ ≈ 0.618 · 1/φ² ≈ 0.382

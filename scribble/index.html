Aa Scribble Font

Turn your kid's handwriting into a real font — then into videos.

Print the handwriting sheet

Two pages: capitals & numbers, then lowercase & symbols. Kids write one character in each box — that's it.

  • Use a dark marker or felt-tip pen. Pencil works but comes out thin and faint. Any colour is fine.
  • Letters sit on the solid line. Tails on g, j, p, q, y hang below it. Keep each letter inside its box.
  • Print at 100% (or "fit to page"). Letter or A4 paper both work.
  • Already made a font here before? Load your font file and skip ahead.

Scan the filled-in sheets

Take a photo of each page from straight above, in good light, with all four black corner squares in the shot.

Tap a letter to leave it out of the font (for example, a messy one you'd rather redo).
